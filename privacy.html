BestBits Factory — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

BestBits Factory ("the App") is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's TikTok account as drafts.

Data we receive from TikTok. When you authorize the App, TikTok provides an access token, a refresh token, your open ID and basic profile information (user.info.basic). The App requests only the scopes needed to upload drafts (video.upload).

How it is used. The tokens are used solely to upload videos you choose to your own TikTok inbox and to check upload status. The App does not read your messages, followers or other content, and does not post anything publicly on its own.

Storage. Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local file readable by the owner alone. No data is stored on any server operated by the App.

Sharing. No data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.

Deletion. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions). Deleting the local token file removes all stored data.

Children. The App is not intended for anyone under 18.
